HypnoTapp Privacy Policy

Last updated September 21, 2026

HypnoTapp is operated by Quantum Nonsense. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. The short version: we collect what the app needs to work, we never sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

What we collect

Account details: your email address and a password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us.

Date of birth: used to confirm you are 18 or older and to work out your zodiac sign, which shapes your first constellation.

Profile and preferences: your Cosmic Name, the goals and moods you choose, favourites, and your routine and reminder time.

Listening activity: which sessions you start and finish, and your place in them, so progress and points can be recorded.

Points and unlocks: the points you earn and anything you unlock with them.

Wellness notes you choose to write: journal entries and reflections, check-in ratings, and progress tracking such as focus areas, beliefs and how tools worked for you.

Constellation progress: the steps you have earned, and your time-zone offset, which is used only to tell which calendar day it is for you.

Diagnostics: if the app crashes we may receive technical details such as device model, system version, app version and the error. Diagnostics never include your journal entries or other wellness notes.

Your wellness notes are private

Journal entries, check-ins, beliefs and reflections are personal. They are stored so you can see them on your devices, protected by access rules that allow only your account to read them, and are never used for advertising or shared with other users.

How we use information

To run the app: signing you in, keeping your progress in sync across devices, calculating points and progress, and showing the content you have unlocked.

To keep accounts secure and to find and fix problems.

We do not use your information to build advertising profiles.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not show third-party advertising.

We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Account data is stored with our hosting and database provider on servers in the United States. Some data is also cached on your device so the app works offline, and sign-in tokens are kept in your device’s secure storage.

We share information only with service providers that host or operate the app on our behalf, under confidentiality obligations, or when the law requires it.

Reminders are scheduled on your own device.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information for as long as you have an account.

You can delete your account at any time from Account (the profile button at the top of Home), then Delete my account. Deletion is permanent: it removes your profile and every record tied to it, including journal entries, check-ins, progress tracking, points, unlocks and constellations. Data cached on your device is cleared at the same time.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or to object to certain uses. To make a request, write to support@quantumnonsense.com from the email address on your account.

Age

HypnoTapp is for adults. You must be 18 or older to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and protected by per-account access controls. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Changes to this policy

If we make a meaningful change we will update the date above and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions, requests or concerns: support@quantumnonsense.com. We read every message and aim to reply within a few days.
