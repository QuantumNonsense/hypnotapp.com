Delete your HypnoTapp account

HypnoTapp is made by Quantum Nonsense. You can delete your account and everything in it at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

From inside the app

  1. Open HypnoTapp and sign in.
  2. Tap the profile button at the top right of Home to open Account.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm. Your session is signed out everywhere and the account is deleted.

If you can no longer open the app

Email alexjamesweiss@gmail.com from the email address on the account with the subject “Delete my HypnoTapp account”. We will confirm it is you before deleting. [We reply within 7 days.]

What is deleted

  • Your profile, email address, date of birth and Cosmic Name
  • Journal entries, reflections and mood check-ins
  • Progress tracking: focus areas, beliefs and tool reflections
  • Points, unlocks, journey progress and your constellations
  • Records of in-app purchases tied to the account
  • Anything cached on your device, which is cleared at the same time

Deletion takes effect immediately. [Copies in our encrypted backups are removed within 30 days.] Nothing is kept for advertising or analytics, because we do not do either.

Purchases

Journeys bought through the App Store or Google Play are managed by that store. Deleting your HypnoTapp account does not refund a purchase, and a deleted account cannot restore one, so restore any purchases to a new account before you delete the old one if you intend to keep them.

Questions

Write to alexjamesweiss@gmail.com. Our Privacy Policy explains what we collect and why.
